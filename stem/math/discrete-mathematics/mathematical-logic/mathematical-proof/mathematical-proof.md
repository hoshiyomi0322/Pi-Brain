# Without Loss of Generality (WLOG)
- ### Symmetry
- ### Example：For any real numbers $x$ and $y$, prove that $x^2 + y^2 \ge 2xy$
    - Symmetry：The inequality is symmetric in $x$ and $y$ ($x^2 + y^2 \ge 2xy \iff y^2 + x^2 \ge 2yx$)
    - WLOG：Assume $x \le y$, and let $y=x+a$ for some $a\ge0$
