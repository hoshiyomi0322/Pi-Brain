# Perfect Number
- ### $n=\sum{f}=2^{k-1} \times \left(2^k-1\right)$

- ### Properties of Perfect Numbers
    - $n=\text{triangular number}$
    - $\text{If }n=\text{even number},~\text{then }n \text{ ends in }6\text{ or }28$
- ### Example：6, 28, 496, 8128
