# Mathematical Notation
- ### Interval Notation
    |Interval|Notation|Definition|
    |:---:|:---:|:---:|
    |Open Interval|$`\left(a,~b\right)`$|$`\{x\mid a<x<b\}`$|
    |Close Interval|$`\left[a,~b\right]`$|$`\{x\mid a\le x\le b\}`$|
    |Half-open Interval|$`\left(a,~b\right]`$|$`\{x\mid a<x\le b\}`$|
    |Half-open Interval|$`\left[a,~b\right)`$|$`\{x\mid a\le x<b\}`$|

# Extremum 
|Notation|Definition|
|:---:|:---:|
|$\max{x}$|Maximum|
|$\min{x}$|Minimum|
|$\mathop{\text{argmax}}{~x}$|Argument of the Maximum|
|$\mathop{\text{argmin}}{~x}$|Argument of the Minimum|
- ### eg
    - $`f(x)=\begin{cases} {f(1)=35}\\{f(2)=69}\\{f(3)=24} \end{cases}`$
    - $\max{f(x)}=69$
    - $\min{f(x)}=24$
    - $\mathop{\text{argmax}}{~f(x)}=2$
    - $\mathop{\text{argmin}}{~f(x)}=3$

# Mathematical Abbreviation
|Abbreviation|Definition|
|:---:|:---:|
|$Q.E.D.$|$`\text{Quod Erat Demonstrandum}`$|
|$LHS$|$\text{Left Hand Side}$|
|$RHS$|$\text{Right Hand Side}$|
|$s.t.$|$\text{subject to}$|
|$s.t.$|$\text{such that}$|
|$e.g.$|$\text{exempli gratia, for example}$|
- ### eg：$`\sin^2{θ}+\cos^2{θ}=1`$
    - $`LHS=\sin^2{θ}+\cos^2{θ}`$
    - $`RHS=1`$
