# Symmetric Encryption
- ### Key

- ### Encrypt：$\text{Plaintext} \xrightarrow{\text{key}} \text{Ciphertext}$
- ### Decrypt：$\text{Ciphertext} \xrightarrow{\text{key}} \text{Plaintext}$
