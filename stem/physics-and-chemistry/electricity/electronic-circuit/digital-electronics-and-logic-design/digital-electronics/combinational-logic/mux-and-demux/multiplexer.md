# $2^n\text{-to-}1$ Multiplexer ($2^n\text{-to-}1$ MUX)
- ### Signal
    ||Number of lines|Lines|
    |:---:|:---:|:---:|
    |**Inputs**|$2^n$|$I_0,~I_1,~\cdots,~I_{2^n-1}$|
    |**Selects**|$n$|$S_0,~S_1,~\cdots,~S_{n-1}$|
    |**Outputs**|$1$|$Y$|
- ### $Y=I_x$
    - $x=(S_{n-1} \times 2^{n-1} ) +\cdots+ (S_1 \times 2^1 )+(S_0 \times 2^0 )$
